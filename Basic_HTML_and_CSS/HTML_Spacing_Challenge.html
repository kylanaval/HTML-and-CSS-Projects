Poem About Dogs

In quiet nights and golden days,
A dog will walk beside your ways.

With loyal heart and gentle eyes,
A faithful friend who never lies.

Through stormy skies and sunshine bright,
They guard your home each day and night.

A wagging tail, a joyful bark,
A little light within the dark.

Forever a Friend

From playful paws to cuddles tight,
They fill our lives with pure delight.

No matter where the road may bend,
A dog will stay until the end.

Oh best companion, brave and true,
The world feels warmer because of you.
