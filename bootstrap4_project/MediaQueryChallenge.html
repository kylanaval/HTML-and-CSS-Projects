Welcome to Kyla's Webpage

Kyla Cartalla is a multifaceted professional whose life and work reflect both academic contribution and personal courage. she is credited as a co‑author on a peer‑reviewed research article titled “Accounting Skills in Practice and Their Impact on Employability: A Curriculum Review in an Autonomous Philippine University,” published in the JPAIR Institutional Research Journal in 2021, demonstrating her involvement in international academic research on education and employability. According to academic profiles, she has authored research with measurable citations, showing engagement in scholarly inquiry.

Before moving abroad, Kyla held a managerial position with a distribution company associated with Nestlé in East Visayas, Philippines, where she developed leadership and operational experience. Seeking opportunities for a greener pasture, she later relocated to Canada to work as a caregiver, courageously transitioning from her previous management role to dedicate herself to supporting individuals and families through caregiving work. This journey highlights her adaptability, professional range, and commitment to personal growth and service.
