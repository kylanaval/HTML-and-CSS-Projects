Favorite Place To Visit

My favorite place to visit is the Eiffel Tower in Paris. There’s something magical about seeing it in person— the way it stands so tall and elegant over the city is unforgettable. Whether it’s sparkling at night or shining under the daytime sky, it always feels special. Being there makes me appreciate the beauty of Paris and its rich culture, and it’s definitely a place I’d love to visit again.
